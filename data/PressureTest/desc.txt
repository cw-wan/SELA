# Pressure Test — Time Series Event Specification

This document describes the events in a pressure test task. Each event is defined by its channel morphologies, temporal phases, and the conditions that distinguish it from other events.

**Channels available:** `[volume, pressure]`

**IMPORTANT**: Each sample should contain only one event: either valid test or lost seal.

---

## 1. Valid Test

**Channels:** `[volume, pressure]`

### Pre-Phase (Context)
**Volume Channel:** Strictly steady (holding).
**Pressure Channel:** Strictly steady at the test target level.

### Main-Phase (Event Morphologies)
This phase is defined by the strict temporal sequence of two sub-patterns: **Drawdown** followed immediately by **Buildup**.

> **Sub-Pattern 1 — Drawdown**
> >> **Volume Channel:** Linear increase (consistent positive slope).
> >> **Pressure Channel:** Drops, with the rate of change decreasing toward zero (i.e., a decelerating drop). May optionally settle into a brief steady stage at the end.

> **Sub-Pattern 2 — Buildup**
> >> **Volume Channel:** Strictly steady (slope ≈ 0) at the same level reached at the end of Drawdown.
> >> **Pressure Channel:** Slight increase (recovery). The rate of increase decelerates (2nd derivative < 0), then pressure transitions into a strictly steady stage (slope ≈ 0).

*Note:* The transition between Drawdown and Buildup must be **seamless** (no temporal gap).

### Post-Phase (Termination)
The event ends when **either** of the following occurs:
- **Pressure Channel:** Increases abruptly.
- **Volume Channel:** Changes (either increase or drop).

---

## 2. Lost Seal

**Channels:** `[volume, pressure]`

### Pre-Phase (Context)
**Volume Channel:** Strictly steady (holding).
**Pressure Channel:** Strictly steady at the test target level.

### Main-Phase (Event Morphologies)
The Volume Channel follows a fixed morphology, while the Pressure Channel exhibits **one of two possible morphologies**.

> **Volume Channel (fixed morphology):**
> >> Linear increase, then transitions into strictly steady (slope ≈ 0).

> **Pressure Channel — Possibility A: Spiky Decay then Steady**
> >> Exhibits significant **downward spikes** — pressure drops abruptly but quickly recovers to **the same level**.
> >> Followed by strictly steady behavior (holding).

> **Pressure Channel — Possibility B: Slow Linear Decline**
> >> Exhibits a steady, **slightly linear decrease** with a small consistent negative slope.
> >> Possible small oscillations around the trend.

### Post-Phase (Termination)
The event ends when **either** of the following occurs on the Volume Channel:
- **Volume Channel:** Drops abruptly, **or**
- **Volume Channel:** Transitions into linear increase.

---

## Differentiation Notes

The two events share an identical Pre-Phase, but differ in their Main-Phase morphologies:

| Aspect | Valid Test | Lost Seal |
|---|---|---|
| Volume during pressure stabilization | Steady at elevated level (Buildup) | Steady at elevated level — but only after a longer/larger linear increase |
| Pressure recovery behavior | **Slight increase** (recovery) decelerating to steady | **No recovery** — either spiky decay back to same level, or slow linear decline |
| Key signature | Pressure rebounds slightly | Pressure either drops in spikes or slowly bleeds down |
